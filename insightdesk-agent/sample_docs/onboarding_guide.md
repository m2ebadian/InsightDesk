# Support Team Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the support team! This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Week 1

Get access to the Orders portal, the warehouse system and the ticketing tool. Shadow a senior agent for at least three days and read the warranty policy and the returns policy.

## Week 2

Start answering tickets in the shipment status queue, since those are the simplest. After that, move on to warranty lookups.

## Response time goals

First response on a new ticket should happen within 4 business hours. Urgent tickets, like a production line that is down, need a response within 1 hour.

## Who to ask

For warranty claims above your approval limit, ask your team lead. For shipping problems, contact the logistics desk. For questions about technical documentation, post in the engineering support channel.
