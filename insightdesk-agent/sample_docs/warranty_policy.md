# Warranty Policy

## Coverage periods

- Standard parts are covered for 12 months from the delivery date.
- Premium and industrial parts are covered for 24 months from the delivery date.
- Refurbished parts are covered for 90 days.

## What is not covered

Damage caused by incorrect installation, physical impact, or using a part outside its rated voltage is not covered. Normal wear on belts, filters and seals is also excluded.

## How to check warranty status

Look up the order in the Orders portal using the order number or the customer email. The warranty end date is shown on the order detail page. If the part was bought through a distributor, ask the customer for the distributor invoice, because those orders are not in our portal.

## Approving a claim

Claims under $500 can be approved by any support agent. Claims between $500 and $2,000 need approval from a team lead. Anything above $2,000 goes to the operations manager.
